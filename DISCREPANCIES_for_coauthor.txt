ITEM 3: FullRequests vs Tables — which has the treatment variable?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
YOUR NOTE:
  "CSV Files/FullRequest has the exemption data."
  (And separately: Tables can be ignored.)

WHAT WE FOUND:
  The FullRequests files for Lists 3 and 4a do NOT contain a Status/decision

  and comments, but they cannot replace the Tables for the Granted/Denied
  outcome without additional parsing.
